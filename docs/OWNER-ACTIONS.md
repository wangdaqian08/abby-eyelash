## 1. Google Business Profile (GBP) — do this first

**Why:** GBP was 28.6% of all local AI citations in a study of about 1.97 million citations, and Gemini uses Google Maps data for local answers. Google says complete, accurate profiles are more likely to show up. Evidence: **High**. Sources: [BrightLocal study](https://www.brightlocal.com/resources/ai-directory-sources/), [Google: improve your local ranking](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091), [Gemini Maps grounding](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/maps-grounding).

**Steps**
- [ ] Create or claim the profile and complete Google's verification.
- [ ] Primary category: **Eyelash salon**. Secondary category: **Nail salon**.
- [ ] Business name, address, phone: copy exactly from `src/data/site.ts`. Website: the new domain.
- [ ] Opening hours: same as the website. Update Google when hours change (public holidays too).
- [ ] Add **each service with its price**, using the same names and prices as the website: Eyelash Extensions (Japanese classic, hybrid, styled, bottom lashes, infill, removal), Lash Lift, Handcrafted Nails (hand-painted nail art), Nail Polish (gel, regular), Nail Extensions (BIAB, Gel-X, infill). How to add: [Google: add or edit services](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9455399).
- [ ] Description (English): who you are, what you do, Burwood location, one or two real facts about your work. No keyword lists, no suburb lists.
- [ ] Photos: real photos of your work and the studio (see the shot list at the end). Add new ones regularly.
- [ ] Booking link: the website booking page.
- [ ] Post an update (new work, availability) every few weeks.
- [ ] Copy your **review link** from the profile and send it to us. It goes on the website ("Leave us a review on Google").

**Time:** about 1.5 hours once, then 10 minutes a month.

**中文说明：** 这是最重要的一步。请认领"Google 商家资料"，主分类选 "Eyelash salon"，次分类选 "Nail salon"。名称、地址、电话必须与网站 `src/data/site.ts` 中完全一致。每项服务都要填写名称和价格，上传真实作品照片，填写营业时间和英文简介。完成后把"评价链接"发给我们。预计 1.5 小时。

## 2. Reviews system — ask everyone, reply to everyone

**Why:** Google says more reviews and positive ratings help local ranking, and recommends replying to reviews. Some engines quote review count and rating when they recommend a business. Evidence: **High** for Google, **Med** for other AI tools. Sources: [Google: improve your local ranking](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091), [Google: manage reviews](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122), [BrightLocal survey](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/), [Dossis Digital AU study](https://dossisdigital.com.au/ai-search-australia/).

**The rules (these are policy, not suggestions)**
- **Ask every client**, the same way, after their appointment.
- **No incentives.** No discounts, free add-ons or prizes for reviews.
- **No gating.** Do not ask only happy clients, and do not send unhappy clients somewhere else ("not happy? email us").
- **No scripts.** Do not tell clients what to write, and do not write reviews for them. Do not set staff quotas.
- **Reply to every review**, personally (no copy-paste template). Reply in Chinese to Chinese reviews. A mix of positive and negative reviews looks more trustworthy; do not delete or hide the critical ones.
- Aim for a **steady flow** (a few per week), not a burst.
- Policy sources: [Google Maps policy: fake engagement](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114); [ACCC: online reviews](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/advertising-and-promotions/online-reviews-for-product-and-services). The ACCC says that if an incentive is offered it must apply to positive and negative reviews alike and be disclosed. We avoid the issue by offering no incentive at all. Evidence: **High** (official).

**Ready-to-use SMS (send the same text to every client, the day after the appointment)**

English:
> Hi [Name], thanks for visiting Abby Eyelash. If you have a minute, we'd appreciate your honest review on Google: [review link]. Thank you! – Abby

中文 (for clients who speak Chinese with you):
> [姓名]你好，感谢光临 Abby Eyelash。如果你方便，欢迎在 Google 上留下你的真实评价：[评价链接]。谢谢！– Abby

Template notes: it does not mention stars, discounts or what to write, and it goes to everyone. Do not add "5-star", "leave us a good review" or any reward.

**Checklist**
- [ ] Put the SMS in your phone's saved messages (EN and ZH versions).
- [ ] Send it after every appointment (or show a QR code of the review link at the counter).
- [ ] Reply to every new review within a few days.

**Time:** 5 minutes setup, then about 5 minutes per week.

**中文说明：** 每位客人都要邀请（不能只邀请满意的客人）；不能给折扣或礼物；不能规定评价内容；不能把不满意的客人引导到别处。所有评价都要回复，中文评价用中文回复，不要用千篇一律的模板。评价数量要持续增长，不要集中一次性邀请。上面的短信可直接使用。依据：Google 政策和澳洲 ACCC 规定。

## 3. Xiaohongshu (RED / 小红书) professional account

**Why:** Burwood has the highest share of Mandarin speakers of any NSW council (19.9%), and 32.8% of residents have Chinese ancestry. RED is a main discovery channel for Chinese-speaking customers. Note: RED blocks Google and AI crawlers, so it works **separately** from the website. It will not help the website show up in ChatGPT or Google. Evidence: **Med** for reaching Chinese customers, **None** for website AI visibility. Sources: [Burwood Community Snapshot](https://www.burwood.nsw.gov.au/files/assets/public/v/2/burwood-community-snapshot-2024.pdf), [RED robots.txt](https://www.xiaohongshu.com/robots.txt), [Lowy Institute survey](https://charts.lowyinstitute.org/features/chinese-communities/topics/media-use-and-news-habits).

**Steps**
- [ ] Create a professional (business) account. Name and bio: studio name, "Burwood 悉尼宝活", services, booking method.
- [ ] Post your own real work photos and short videos. Show before/after and the finished set.
- [ ] Use Chinese service terms in titles and text: 美睫, 嫁接睫毛, 睫毛翘 (睫毛翘睫), 手绘美甲, 光疗甲, 延长甲 (also BIAB, Gel-X).
- [ ] Add the **location tag** (Burwood studio) on every post.
- [ ] Include price and duration where you can, matching the website.
- [ ] Post about once a week.
- [ ] Clients may post their own notes if they want to. **Do not pay for notes, give freebies for them, or buy fake notes or likes.** Fake or paid reviews can break platform rules and Australian consumer law.
- [ ] Send us your RED profile link for the website.

**Time:** about 2 hours setup, then about 1 hour a week.

**中文说明：** 建立小红书专业号。发布真实作品；标题和正文使用中文服务词（美睫、嫁接睫毛、睫毛翘、手绘美甲、光疗甲、延长甲）；每篇都加上店铺定位（Burwood / 悉尼宝活）。不要花钱买笔记、点赞或"好评"，也不要用礼品换笔记。小红书与网站是两个独立渠道，在小红书发帖不会让网站出现在 Google 或 ChatGPT 中，但能直接触达华人客人。

## 4. Apple Business Connect and Bing Places

**Why:** Apple Business Connect controls how you appear in Apple Maps, Wallet and Siri; Apple lists its location management feature for Australia. Bing Places feeds Bing, Maps and Copilot, and can import your GBP in minutes. Evidence: **Med** (Apple), **Low–Med** (Bing; mostly vendor guides). Sources: [Apple newsroom (AU)](https://www.apple.com/au/newsroom/2023/01/introducing-apple-business-connect/), [Apple Support: Apple Business features](https://support.apple.com/en-nz/126603), [Whitespark: Bing Places guide](https://whitespark.ca/guides/the-ultimate-guide-to-your-bing-places-listing/).

**Steps**
- [ ] **Apple Business Connect:** sign in with an Apple ID, claim the business, enter NAP exactly as in `site.ts`, hours, website, category, photos.
- [ ] **Bing Places:** choose **import from Google Business Profile**, then check every field matches GBP and `site.ts`.
- [ ] Do this *after* GBP is finished, so there is only one set of facts to copy.

**Time:** about 30 minutes each.

**中文说明：** 先完成 Google 商家资料，再认领 Apple Business Connect（苹果地图/Siri），并在 Bing Places 选择"从 Google 商家资料导入"。名称、地址、电话、营业时间必须与网站完全一致。各约 30 分钟。

## 5. Facebook page and Yelp AU

**Why:** Facebook was the #3 most-cited source in Google AI Overviews and AI Mode in the BrightLocal data. Yelp matters a lot for ChatGPT in US data, but Yelp scaled back outside North America in 2016, so Australian coverage is thin. Claiming it is cheap, but do not expect much. Evidence: **Med** (Facebook), **Low for Australia** (Yelp). Sources: [BrightLocal study](https://www.brightlocal.com/resources/ai-directory-sources/), [Steady Demand: ChatGPT local results](https://www.steadydemand.com/chatgpts-local-results-arent-coming-from-foursquare-and-probably-never-really-were/), [TechCrunch on Yelp's retreat](https://techcrunch.com/2016/11/03/yelp-lays-off-175-in-sales-and-marketing-as-it-retrenches-internationally/).

**Steps**
- [ ] Facebook business page: NAP exactly as `site.ts`, hours, website, booking link, a few real photos. Post occasionally.
- [ ] Yelp AU (yelp.com.au): search for your studio, claim it or add it. Same NAP, hours, photos.
- [ ] Send us the Facebook and Instagram links for the website.

**Time:** about 1 hour in total.

**中文说明：** 建立 Facebook 商家主页，并在 Yelp 澳洲站认领店铺，信息与网站完全一致。Facebook 的 AI 引用较多；Yelp 在澳洲覆盖较弱，认领即可，不必投入更多。

## 6. Earn third-party mentions

**Why:** "Best of" lists and mentions on other sites ranked #1 among AI factors in an expert survey (n=47, an opinion survey, not a measurement). You cannot buy these cleanly; you earn them. Evidence: **Med**. Sources: [Whitespark Local Search Ranking Factors](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/), [The HOTH: AI cites listicles](https://thehoth.com/blog/ai-cites-listicles/).

**Steps** (as opportunities come up, no fixed schedule)
- [ ] Reply to local bloggers or Sydney lifestyle sites that write "best lash / nail in Burwood / Inner West" roundups, and offer to be included.
- [ ] Sydney Chinese-language media and community sites: offer a real story or studio profile.
- [ ] Ask your lash or nail training academy and product suppliers whether they have an alumni or "find a technician" list.
- [ ] Do **not** publish your own "Top 10 salons in Burwood" page ranking yourself first. AI tools often cite these but recommend competitors.
- [ ] Do **not** pay for fake mentions or reviews.

**Time:** ongoing, about 30 minutes a month.

**中文说明：** 争取被本地"最佳店铺"榜单、悉尼华文媒体、培训学院或供应商名单收录。不要自己发布"Burwood 十大店铺"并把自己排第一，也不要购买虚假推荐。

## Not now: Fresha marketplace listing (decision D5)

Fresha lets people search and book salons inside ChatGPT and Claude (announced August 2026). We are **not** doing it now: you would have a second booking system next to your Google Calendar, which risks double-bookings, and there is no data showing it helps you get recommended. **Revisit after the website has launched** and you know how bookings are going. Evidence: **Med** that listings can be surfaced, **None** for ranking effect. Source: [Compare the Cloud: Fresha on ChatGPT and Claude](https://www.comparethecloud.net/news/fresha-becomes-first-beauty-marketplace-to-go-live-on-both-chatgpt-and-claude).
