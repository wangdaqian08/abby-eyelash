2. **Show infill prices by week on the price page** (2 wk / 3 wk / 4 wk), not "50–85% of last price". Laila Lash & Beauty (https://lailalashbeauty.com.au/pricing/) and Kim Sun Young ("Infill from $80 (within 2 weeks)") do this; it removes the "how much will it cost me?" DM.
3. **Bilingual labels on each service name ("Classic Set | 日式自然款")**, the way KOI Nail&Eyelash on Burwood Rd does on Fresha (https://www.fresha.com/a/koi-nail-eyelash-burwood-170-burwood-road-wbbjmdok). Laila's Instagram bio carries 🇦🇺🇯🇵🇨🇳 (https://www.instagram.com/laila.lashbeauty/). Abby already has the language; competitors mostly do not.
4. **State the deposit and cancellation policy in writing on the booking page**, as Lash Blossom does ($50 non-refundable, 24 h, same-day forfeits: https://www.lashblossom.com.au/deposit). Abby's $20 WeChat deposit and 48 h rule exist but should be printed where the client books.
5. **A small, concrete guarantee instead of a discount**: Lash Bunny's "free three day touch up after every appointment" (https://www.lashbunnysydney.com.au/) and WithUBeauty's cheaper "SECOND visit full classic set $70" (Fresha Strathfield listing) drive repeat bookings without a first-visit price cut.

## 5. What not to copy

- **Unsourced review totals ("1900+ five-star reviews", Laila).** The ACCC's online-review guidance treats misleading or unverifiable review claims and selective publication as misleading conduct (https://www.accc.gov.au/business/advertising-and-promotions/online-reviews-for-product-and-services); Citymove paid a penalty for testimonials it claimed were genuine (https://www.accc.gov.au/media-release/accc-releases-guidance-about-online-product-reviews). Show a live Google/Fresha count with a link, or nothing.
- **"Non-refundable" deposit wording with no cost basis (Lash Blossom's $50 on every infill).** Law-firm summaries of ACL say cancellation charges must be a reasonable estimate of loss, not a penalty, and that labelling a deposit non-refundable can mislead when the business itself cancels (https://sprintlaw.com.au/articles/deposit-vs-cancellation-fee-australia/). Abby's $20 is proportionate; keep it, say when it is refunded (artist cancels, 48 h notice).
- **Chain-style multi-location service menus and 40-minute "full set" slots (Coco).** The 1-star Google review shows what happens when policy differs between technicians. One artist, one menu is Abby's advantage; do not pad the menu.
- **Westfield walk-in model (Divas Brow).** Depends on foot traffic and seven staff; a home studio cannot match the slot density, so do not price against $149 classic.
- **Placeholder prices ("from $0", WithUBeauty) or inconsistent menus across pages (Wonderlash, Drawing).** They read as bait and lose the price-comparison click.
- **Eye-only close-ups as the whole gallery (Lash Bunny).** Per the user's own note, face-framed photos convert better for Abby; use real clients with consent, no stock or retouched before/after.
- **Instagram-only presence with no bookable site (Lavish Lashes by Merna, Lady Lash's unofficial price domain).** Followers are not bookings; do not chase follower count before the Fresha/GBP basics.
